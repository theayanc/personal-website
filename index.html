Common questions

How does an engagement typically start?

It begins with a free discovery call where I assess your current data stack, understand your pain points, and identify quick wins. From there, I propose a tailored roadmap.

What cloud platforms do you work with?

I work across AWS, Azure, and GCP, with deep expertise in Snowflake, Databricks, and modern data stack tools like Airflow, dbt, and Spark.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A focused audit can take 2-3 weeks. A full architecture redesign or migration typically runs 2-4 months with clear milestones throughout.

Do you work with startups or enterprises?

Both. I've helped early-stage startups set up their first data infrastructure and supported enterprise teams managing petabyte-scale systems.

Can you work with our existing team?

Absolutely. I integrate seamlessly with your engineering and data teams, providing hands-on collaboration and knowledge transfer so your team owns the solution long-term.
