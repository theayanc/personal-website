Common questions

What type of companies do you work with?

I partner with enterprises looking to scale their data architecture, startups building from the ground up, and companies seeking to optimize their cloud data costs and operational efficiency.

What are your core areas of expertise?

My expertise spans Cloud Data Engineering, Data Architecture, Modern Data Stack implementations, Cost Optimization, and building robust, scalable data platforms.

Do you provide end-to-end implementation or just consulting?

Both. Depending on the engagement, I can act as an advisory Fractional Data Architect or work hands-on with your team to deliver end-to-end implementations.

How can you help reduce cloud data costs?

I analyze your current architecture and pipelines to identify inefficiencies, optimize compute and storage usage, implement better data modeling, and introduce performance tuning to significantly cut costs.

Do you offer training or upskilling for corporate teams?

Yes, I provide corporate training and mentorship for engineering teams, helping them build strong fundamentals, adopt architectural thinking, and elevate their capabilities to maintain the systems we build.
